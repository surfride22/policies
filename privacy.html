Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Property Logic RE Internal Tools ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it to integrate with Google services on your own behalf.

Who Uses This App

The App is an internal tool used by Tommy Fry, broker/owner of Property Logic RE, to assist with broker price opinion (BPO) and real estate research workflows. It is not offered to the general public.

Information We Access

When you authorize the App, it requests access to the following Google account data on your own account only:

  • Google Drive — read and write documents, spreadsheets, and folders you create or choose
  • Google Docs and Sheets — create, read, and modify documents you create or open with the App
  • Gmail — read, send, and label messages in the authorized account
  • Google Calendar — create and modify calendar events in the authorized account
  • Apps Script external requests — allow scripts you run to reach authorized APIs

How We Use Information

All information accessed via Google APIs stays on the authorized user's own machine and Google account. The App does not transmit, store, sell, or share your Google data with any third party. No analytics, tracking, or telemetry is collected.

Data Storage

The App stores an OAuth refresh token locally on the user's own computer (in ~/.config/google-docs-mcp/token.json) to avoid repeated sign-in. No other personal data is persisted by the App.

Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties. The App communicates only with Google's APIs under your own credentials.

Your Choices

You can revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also logs the App out.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.
