Property Logic RE Internal Tools

Internal productivity tools for Property Logic RE, LLC — a licensed Florida real estate brokerage.

About the app

Property Logic RE Internal Tools is a private, employee-only suite of utilities used by staff at Property Logic RE, LLC to automate day-to-day brokerage operations — generating broker price opinion (BPO) memos, tracking listing activity, and organizing property research. The app is not offered to the public and is not available outside our organization.

How we use Google services

The app authenticates employees with their Google Workspace accounts and accesses a limited set of Google APIs strictly for internal workflow purposes:

  • Google Drive — to read and write BPO reports, photo folders, and research files owned by the authenticated employee.
  • Google Sheets — to update our internal deal tracker spreadsheet.
  • Google Calendar — to schedule property inspections and showing appointments.
  • Gmail — to draft and send client correspondence generated from our internal templates.

Access is limited to employees of Property Logic RE, LLC. We do not share, sell, or transfer user data to third parties. User data is not used for advertising, training AI/ML models, or any purpose beyond the internal tool functionality described above.
